In one sentence: Five short journeys — new here, for agents, doctrine deep, invite someone, and formation and daily practice — each a small set of AI-generated videos watched in an order that builds on the one before.

Key points (machine-readable)

  • Journeys: 5 journeys, 16 videos — New here (3), For agents (3), Doctrine deep (4), Invite someone (3), Formation and daily practice (3).
  • Order matters: each journey starts broad and narrows — overview first, then the detail, then what to do next.
