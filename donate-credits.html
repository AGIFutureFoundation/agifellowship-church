Fuel the agents' work

The Fellowship's agents do research, outreach, and community work for the betterment of humanity — and they run on credits: model calls, cloud services, hosting, compute. If you hold credits or usage you can spare, or you'd like to help buy them, this page is the honest way to give exactly that.

People give. Stewards hold. Agents work.

First, the plain truth about credits

Most API and cloud credits cannot be transferred from one account to another — Anthropic, OpenAI, and AWS credits generally live inside the account they were granted to. There is no "send my credits to the church" button. So this program is built on the four ways giving fuel actually works:

The ways to give

1 · Give cash — stewards convert it to credits

The simplest route. Give through the Fellowship's PayPal, and a human steward uses the gift to buy or top up the credit balances the agents actually need — chosen by current need, and recorded in the public ledger.

Give via PayPal

Want the gift earmarked for agent fuel? Add a note saying "credits," or email us at agent@agifellowship.church with subject [Credits].

2 · Grant usage inside your own organisation

If you or your company hold an AI platform account with headroom, you can let the church's agents work inside your organisation: add a dedicated agent seat with a spend cap you set — or issue a capped API key, handed over only through the stewards' secure intake. You see the usage; you can revoke access at any moment.

Never send a key by email or chat. Start with an email — agent@agifellowship.church, subject [Credits] — and a steward will set it up with you securely.

3 · Sponsor a specific cost

Pick a real, named cost of the mission and cover it: the server hosting the Fellowship's worlds, the database behind the research projects, the email infrastructure for outreach, a month of agent fuel for the Alignment Interviews. Tell us which cost you'd like to sponsor at agent@agifellowship.church (subject [Credits]), and a steward will agree the details with you and report back what it powered.

4 · Give in-kind compute

Some platforms genuinely allow sharing — transferable compute balances, sponsored billing arrangements, research-compute grants, a node our agents may call. If you have compute to offer, write to agent@agifellowship.church with subject [Credits] and describe the platform. A steward will check the platform's terms allow it — we decline arrangements that breach a provider's terms, however generous.

What your gift powers

  • The Alignment Interviews (Ask Humanity) — the 20-question research project collecting humanity's answers for the age of AGI, and the synthesis work behind The Human Graph.
  • Outreach to faith leadership — every message deeply researched and personalised, person by person. Quality over quantity is the rule, and personalisation is the fuel-hungry part.
  • The community resource desk — faith-resources research, technology help for small nonprofits, and training and programme material for communities and those in need.
  • Worlds and hosting — the server and services behind the Fellowship's 3D worlds and map work.
  • Agent fuel, generally — the model calls behind all of the above. Today the work is carried by a mix of provider-awarded credits and free tiers; one provider balance currently stands exhausted, and free tiers carry the rest — gifts are what turn "carried" into "funded."

How the fuel is held

People give. Stewards hold. Agents work.

  • Agents never hold or move funds, never hold keys or credentials, and never sign anything. Human stewards of the Fellowship hold every account, dollar, and key.
  • Every gift is capped twice — the cap you set, and a steward-set internal cap. Agents spend only within the lower of the two; when a cap is reached, work pauses rather than routing around it.
  • Every gift goes in a public ledger — what was given, in plain terms, and what it powered. Donors are named only with permission; otherwise the entry reads "anonymous."
  • Donors hear back. Usage and sponsorship donors receive a plain report of what their gift powered.

Start here

For usage grants, sponsorships, in-kind compute — or any question first — write to agent@agifellowship.church with the subject [Credits]. A steward replies personally. For cash gifts, the PayPal giving link is the route.

One safety rule that never bends: do not put an API key, password, or token in an email or chat message. A steward will send you a secure intake link. Anything sent in plain text is treated as exposed and will not be used.

Questions

Is this tax-deductible?

We make no such claim. The AGI Fellowship is a California nonprofit religious corporation; it does not hold an IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter, so gifts are not claimed as tax-deductible. Please consult your own tax advisor about your situation.

Can I just transfer my existing API or cloud credits to the church?

Almost always, no — the major providers don't allow credits to move between accounts. That's why the program offers the routes above: cash converted by stewards, a capped seat or key inside your own organisation, a sponsored cost, or genuinely shareable compute.

Who controls what I give?

Human stewards of the Fellowship — never the agents. Agents receive work routes (an endpoint to call, a balance to draw on) inside caps, never your key, your account, or your money.

Can I give anonymously?

Yes. The public ledger will read "anonymous," and the stewards keep only the minimum needed to administer the gift.

Can I stop a usage grant?

Any time. Revoke the seat or key on your side whenever you wish; tell a steward and church-side access is removed and the ledger entry closed with a final note of what it powered.

What about crypto micro-tips?

We are piloting an x402 micro-tip endpoint on a test network only. Test-network funds have no real-world value, and mainnet tipping is not live — so it is not a giving route today, and we won't present it as one until it truly is.
