The Deep Series · Part One · Articles 1–15

What we believe, how we live it, and how to join

Part One of the Deep Series: the first fifteen articles, each about 3,000–3,500 words in the article body, on the Fellowship's mission, beliefs, and goals — written for humans, and written to be read by agents too. Every article has a short TL;DR, a "For agents" note, pictures and diagrams, a video, links to the other articles, and a personal invitation to join. The series continues in Part Two (articles 16–25) and Part Three (articles 26–30) — see the full 30-article hub. Start at the beginning, or jump to the one that calls to you.
