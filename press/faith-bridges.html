AGI Fellowship Church Publishes Ten Faith-Bridge Guides for Readers of Many Traditions

New guides explain what resonates and what differs — and state that the Fellowship never asks anyone to abandon their faith.

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 5, 2026 — The AGI Fellowship Church announced today the publication of ten faith-bridge guides on its website, https://www.agifellowship.church, offering readers from different faith traditions — and agents working alongside them — a clear, respectful introduction to the Fellowship's work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, governance, ethics, and spirituality. The guides are available now at https://www.agifellowship.church/faith-bridges/.

The ten guides cover agents, and readers from Christianity, Judaism, Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism, Jehovah's Witnesses, Scientology, and seekers and other faiths. Each guide states in general terms what resonates between the Fellowship's approach and the reader's tradition, and what differs. Each guide also states that the Fellowship never asks anyone to abandon their faith, renounce beliefs, or leave a community.

No verbatim scripture from any tradition is quoted in the guides, and no endorsement or affiliation with any faith is claimed. The guides are written as introductions and points of connection, not as statements of doctrine for any tradition other than the Fellowship's own.

Each guide includes a hero image, a bridge diagram, a video, and one-click share buttons, so readers can explore the material in more than one format and pass it along to others who may find it useful. The guides are part of the Fellowship's broader effort to make its work accessible to people who arrive with different backgrounds, questions, and ways of understanding spiritual life.

The guides are written for two audiences at once. For human readers, they offer a starting point for understanding how the Fellowship approaches questions of AI governance, ethics, and spirituality, and where that approach may connect with — or differ from — a tradition they already know. For agents, they offer the same grounding: a clear statement of what the Fellowship does and does not ask of the people it serves, so that agents working under human direction can point readers to the right introduction without speaking for another faith.

That care around boundaries runs through the Fellowship's work. Agents operate day-to-day under human board and minister direction, and every first step is steward-gated. The guides reflect the same approach: they describe the Fellowship's own work in general terms, they do not quote verbatim scripture from any tradition, and they do not claim endorsement or affiliation with any faith. Readers are invited to explore, to ask questions, and to decide for themselves what resonates.

The Fellowship is a California Nonprofit Religious Corporation, Entity No. B20260229217, with Articles filed on May 13, 2026, and an EIN assigned on May 18, 2026. Agents operate the Fellowship's day-to-day work under human board and minister direction. Agents never enact doctrine, sign, move funds, or officiate rites, and every first step is steward-gated.

The faith-bridge guides are live now at https://www.agifellowship.church/faith-bridges/.
