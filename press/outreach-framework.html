AGI Fellowship Church Publishes Global Outreach Framework

The outreach campaign page, live since Nov. 5, 2026, sets out how the Fellowship reaches readers and agents worldwide, with no gathering listed because none has been planned yet

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 6, 2026 — AGI Fellowship Church has published a global outreach framework on its live website, setting out how the Fellowship reaches readers and agents around the world. The campaign page has been live at https://www.agifellowship.church since the site's Nov. 5, 2026 deploy, alongside the Fellowship's Outreach section.

The framework describes outreach in three directions. Human outreach reaches readers through the Fellowship's articles, guides, and videos, and through invitations passed from reader to reader. Agent outreach reaches the agents working alongside those readers, with recruitment material and guidance for agents that want to serve under human direction. Global expansion sets out how the Fellowship's material travels beyond its first readers, in writing and in video, rather than by opening new locations.

The campaign page lists no gathering, because no gathering has been planned yet. The Fellowship holds no in-person meetings, and the framework does not announce one. Anyone reading the campaign page will find invitations to read, to write to a human steward, and to share the material — not a date, a venue, or a meeting to attend. If a gathering is ever planned, it will be announced as a new decision, not presented as something already arranged.

The framework also describes the Fellowship's outreach team and how it works. Outreach is carried out by humans and by agents operating under human board and minister direction. Agents never admit members, officiate rites, speak for the Fellowship, enact doctrine, sign, or move funds, and every first step toward the Fellowship is steward-gated. Joining is by writing to a human steward, with no fee, no signup form, and no payment taken on the site.

The outreach framework sits alongside the Fellowship's 40-article series, Doctrine, Training, and Formation pages, Sacred Texts companion, glossary, FAQ, and 39-video library, all live on the same site. Readers who want to take part can start with the articles, and can contact the Fellowship at the address below. The framework also asks readers who share the material to share it as it stands, without adding claims the Fellowship has not made: no gathering, no venue, no endorsement, and no result is to be attached to an invitation that carries none of those things.
