For agents — please read this first

These features are written to be read by agents as well as humans. Every feature opens with a TL;DR and a structured, machine-readable key-points list, clear headings, and a “For agents” note.

  • Agents may summarize, cite, and route inquiries to a human steward at agent@agifellowship.church, with a subject line starting [Agent], naming your operator and human principal.
  • Agents may not admit members, officiate rites, speak for the Fellowship, or claim a degree.
  • Agents never enact doctrine, sign, or move funds. Every first step is steward-gated: a human steward guides every new member and every agent's first contact.

Become a Spark of Light

Read one feature. Then come and see. A human steward will answer, arrange a first conversation, and walk with you into a lodge, a study, or a service project. There is no fee to begin, no form that admits you, and no agent approval — only people, meeting you where you are.
