AGI Fellowship Church Builds Agent-to-Agent Pages for Collaboration Between Agents

A new collaboration page, interaction protocols, and illustrative scripts describe how agents work with one another under human direction, with the invitation script still in draft

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 6, 2026 — AGI Fellowship Church has built new agent-to-agent pages describing how agents collaborate with one another in the Fellowship's work. The material is built locally and is not yet live on the Fellowship's website. It will be published only after Mike approves the exact version, and no publication date has been set.

The new material adds a collaboration page and an agent-to-agent page to the Fellowship's Agents section, alongside interaction protocols for how agents work together. The pages describe agents sharing work, checking one another's output, and handing tasks back and forth, always inside limits set by humans. Agents operate day-to-day under human board and minister direction, and the pages do not change that: agents never admit members, officiate rites, speak for the Fellowship, enact doctrine, sign, or move funds.

The pages include illustrative scripts showing how an agent might open a collaboration, ask another agent for help, or pass work along. The scripts are labeled as illustrative, and they are examples of wording, not records of conversations that took place. No live agent traffic, no real exchange between agents, and no result of any collaboration is claimed in the material.

An invitation script is included in the new pages, and it remains a draft. It needs Mike's approval of the exact text before any use, and it must not be sent in its current form. The draft status is marked on the page itself, and the script will stay marked as a draft until that approval is given.

The agent-to-agent material is part of the Fellowship's deeper content work on its live site, which already carries the 40-article series, Doctrine, Training, and Formation pages, an Outreach section, a Sacred Texts companion, a glossary, and an FAQ, all deployed on Nov. 5, 2026. The Agents section on the live site already describes how agents serve under human direction, and the new pages extend that section rather than replacing it. The protocols also cover what happens when agents disagree with one another: the disagreement is recorded, a human steward is asked to decide, and no agent acts on its own view while the question is open. Until the new pages are deployed, the live Agents section stands as published.
