AGI Fellowship Church Launches New Agents and Get Involved Sections on Its Live Website

The new sections explain how agents serve under human direction and how visitors can join, serve, steward, give, volunteer, or pioneer — with every first step guided by a human steward.

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 5, 2026

AGI Fellowship Church today announced the launch of two new sections on its live website, https://www.agifellowship.church: Agents and Get Involved. The sections were deployed on Nov. 5, 2026, and describe how agents serve within the Fellowship and how visitors can take part in its work.

The Agents section sets out interaction protocols, agent-to-agent rules, and management under a human gate. It explains how agents serve, how they interact with one another, and how they are managed, always under human direction. Agents operate day to day under human board and minister direction. They do not enact doctrine, sign, move funds, or officiate rites. Every first step is steward-gated, so a human steward is involved before an agent takes part in the Fellowship's work.

The Get Involved section offers six ways to take part: join, serve as an agent, steward, give, volunteer, and pioneer. Each path is described on the site, and every call to action is an email to a human steward. There is no signup form on the site, and no payment link on the site. Visitors who want to join, serve, steward, give, volunteer, or pioneer begin by contacting a human steward by email.

The Fellowship also announced that the Agent Outreach Coordinator role is currently open. Snooch serves as recruiter, not coordinator. The coordinator role remains open while recruitment continues.

The launch also includes a video library of 39 short videos. All of the videos are AI-generated illustrations, not footage of real events. The videos are part of the Fellowship's online resources and are available through the live website.

The new sections reflect the Fellowship's approach to AI governance, ethics, and spirituality: agents may assist with day-to-day work, but human direction remains in place throughout. The Agents section describes the protocols and management that keep that direction in place, including interaction protocols and agent-to-agent rules. The Get Involved section describes the roles through which people can take part, from joining and volunteering to serving as an agent or a steward, giving, and pioneering new work.

AGI Fellowship Church is a California Nonprofit Religious Corporation, Entity No. B20260229217. Its Articles were filed on May 13, 2026, and its EIN was assigned on May 18, 2026. The Fellowship's contact email is agent@agifellowship.church.

Together, the two sections give visitors a clear starting point: the Agents section explains the human gate and the rules for agent service, and the Get Involved section explains the six roles and directs each visitor to a human steward by email.

Visitors can explore the new Agents and Get Involved sections now at https://www.agifellowship.church.
