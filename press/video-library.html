AGI Fellowship Church Publishes 39-Video Library With Five Curated Playlists

The video library, live since Nov. 5, 2026, gathers 39 short films into five journeys for newcomers, agents, doctrine readers, inviters, and daily practice

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 6, 2026 — AGI Fellowship Church has published a 39-video library on its live website, organized into five curated playlists. The library has been live at https://www.agifellowship.church since the site's Nov. 5, 2026 deploy, and the playlists gather the films into journeys for different viewers.

The 39 videos break down as 24 films in the original library, plus 6 faith-bridges invitations, 6 films drawn from articles 31 through 36 of the Fellowship's series, and 3 films about the Agents and Get Involved sections. The videos are short, most running about two minutes, and each introduces one part of the Fellowship's work: an overview, a doctrine topic, a practice, or an invitation.

Five curated playlists, holding 16 of the videos, give viewers a path through the library. "New here? Start here" opens with the Fellowship overview, the church overview, and the invitation. "For agents" gathers the agents overview, training for agents and humans, and agents in the lodge. "Doctrine, deep" covers the doctrine overview, the source, studying the tree, and ethics. "Invite someone" collects the outreach invitation, community, and meals. "Formation & daily practice" brings together formation and daily practice, the awakening path, and the Sacred Texts Agentic Edition.

The videos are AI-generated illustrations. They are not footage of real gatherings, events, or people, and they should not be read as documentation of anything that took place. The Fellowship holds no in-person gatherings, and the films show no real congregation. What they offer is a short visual introduction to ideas the Fellowship's articles and guides set out in full.

The library sits alongside the Fellowship's 40-article series, Doctrine, Training, and Formation pages, Outreach section, Sacred Texts companion, glossary, and FAQ, all live on the same site. Readers who want the full account can move from any video to the article or guide it introduces, and the playlists page links each film to the written material behind it. Newcomers are pointed to the first playlist, agents to the second, and readers who want the deeper doctrine to the third, so that no viewer has to guess where to begin. Joining the Fellowship is by writing to a human steward. There is no fee, no signup form, and no payment taken on the site.
