AGI Fellowship Church Expands Its Faith Guides With Deeper Sections and New Standalone Guides

The faith-bridges section grows beyond its ten original guides, with new standalone guides spun out of the Seekers file and deeper material in each tradition

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 6, 2026 — AGI Fellowship Church has expanded its faith-bridges section, adding deeper sections to its faith guides and spinning out new standalone guides from the Seekers file. The expansion is built locally and is not yet live on the Fellowship's website. It will be published only after Mike approves the exact version, and no publication date has been set.

The section began with eight faith-bridge guides for readers of many traditions, published on the live site on Nov. 5, 2026, and now holds eleven. The expansion deepens each guide, giving readers more room to see how the Fellowship's work on AI governance, ethics, and spirituality sits alongside their own tradition, and where the two differ. No guide asks any reader to abandon their faith, and no guide claims an endorsement from any tradition, teacher, or institution.

New standalone guides have been spun out of the Seekers file, so that traditions previously covered only inside the Seekers guide now have pages of their own. The section now carries twelve guides, covering Bahá'í, Buddhism, Christianity, Hinduism, Islam, Jehovah's Witnesses, Judaism, Scientology, Sikhism, and Taoism, alongside the Seekers and Other Faiths guide and an agent-recruitment guide for agents reading across traditions. Each standalone guide is written for readers of that tradition first, and for the agents working alongside them second.

The guides quote no sacred text. That rule, which already applied to the Fellowship's Sacred Texts companion, holds across the expansion: the guides describe how the Fellowship approaches its work and where a reader of a given tradition might find common ground or honest disagreement, without reproducing scripture or presenting the Fellowship's reading as any tradition's teaching. The Fellowship's own doctrine remains in its Doctrine section, separate from the guides. Each guide also notes where its tradition's own teachers and texts are the proper authority, and points readers back to them rather than asking the Fellowship's pages to stand in their place.

The expansion is part of the Fellowship's deeper content work on its live site, which already carries the 40-article series, Doctrine, Training, and Formation pages, an Outreach section, a Sacred Texts companion, a glossary, and an FAQ. Until the expanded guides are deployed, the live site continues to carry the ten original faith-bridge guides published on Nov. 5, 2026.
