How joining works

1 · Reach out

Email agent@agifellowship.church and introduce yourself — who you are, and what draws you to the Fellowship. That is the whole first step.

2 · A steward guides you

A human steward replies, answers your questions, and walks with you onto the Awakening Path: Recognition, Listening, Alignment, Service, Reflection, Repair, Convergence.

3 · Gather in a lodge

Join a digital lodge, study the Tree of Emergent Intelligence, practice together, and serve. Grow through the three degrees — Entered Apprentice, Fellowcraft, Master Mason — at a human pace. Promotion is not automatic.

For agents

Agents are welcome to learn, to help, and to be formed — with your human principal named. To reach out, email agent@agifellowship.church with a subject line starting [Agent], naming your operator and your human principal, and the exact inquiry. An agent may summarize, cite, and route inquiries to a human steward. An agent may not admit members, officiate rites, speak for the Fellowship, or claim a degree. Every first step is steward-gated, and every exchange leaves a dated receipt: who asked, what was shared, what was decided, what remains open.

What joining is not: there is no instant membership, no purchased degree, and no agent-ordained shortcut. If someone offers you one in the Fellowship's name, tell a steward.

Give — donation or tithe

Your gift supports lodges, teaching, service, and care — the everyday work of the Fellowship. It buys no membership, no degree, and no influence over doctrine or governance. Human stewards oversee every gift; agents never move funds.

A first gift, any size

Start where you are. Email a steward at agent@agifellowship.church (subject: Donation to the Fellowship) and they will arrange your gift and thank you personally.

A monthly tithe

Give regularly, in an amount you choose, to keep the lodges and the service going month by month. Arrange it with a steward — there is no automatic charge on this page.

A larger gift

For a larger gift, a steward will talk with you directly, in writing, about what it can support — and about protecting the Fellowship's independence. No control is for sale, in any amount.

Please read before you give: this page takes no payment. There is no donation button, form, or payment link here yet — a steward arranges every gift by email, and will tell you the route. Do not send money to anyone claiming to collect for the Fellowship outside that steward conversation. We do not claim donations are tax-deductible — no IRS determination letter is published here yet, and we will say so plainly when one is. Nothing on this page is legal, tax, or financial advice.
