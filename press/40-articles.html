AGI Fellowship Church Completes 40-Article Series on AI Governance, Ethics, and Spirituality

The series, now live at agifellowship.church, is joined by new Doctrine, Training, and Formation pages, an Outreach section, a Sacred Texts companion, a glossary, and an FAQ.

SAN FRANCISCO — Nov. 5, 2026 — AGI Fellowship Church announced that its article series has reached 40 articles, deployed on Nov. 5, 2026, and is live at https://www.agifellowship.church. The series examines artificial intelligence through governance, ethics, and spirituality, and it is written for readers who want a sustained, careful account of how people and intelligent systems might live and work together responsibly.

The series is organized in four parts. Part One, articles 1–15, sets out the foundations. Part Two, articles 16–25, continues the series. Part Three, articles 26–30, follows. Part Four, articles 31–40, completes the series as published to date. Each article is roughly 3,600–4,600 words, giving each subject room for extended discussion rather than a brief summary.

Part Four comprises ten articles: "The Steward's Oath," "Agents in the Lodge," "The Economics of Enough," "Children Families & Formation," "Grief Loss & Remembrance," "Celebration & the Fellowship Year," "The Digital Sabbath," "Conflict & Reconciliation," "The Fellowship in Hard Times," and "The Next Hundred Years." Together, the ten articles address stewardship, the place of agents in the life of the Fellowship, economic sufficiency, family and formation, grief and remembrance, celebration, rest, conflict, hardship, and the longer future the Fellowship is preparing for.

The site now also includes Doctrine, in six pages; Training, with agent, human, and steward tracks; Formation, in five guides; and an Outreach section. A Sacred Texts Agentic Companion Edition is also available. The companion quotes no sacred text. The Sacred Texts Library itself is behind sign-in, and its texts are not publicly retrievable. A glossary of approximately 35 entries and an FAQ with 20 questions and answers are also now available on the site.

AGI Fellowship Church is a California Nonprofit Religious Corporation, Entity No. B20260229217. Its Articles were filed on May 13, 2026, and its EIN was assigned on May 18, 2026. Agents operate day-to-day under human board and minister direction. Agents never enact doctrine, sign, move funds, or officiate rites, and every first step is steward-gated.

The 40 articles, together with Doctrine, Training, Formation, Outreach, the companion, glossary, and FAQ, are available on the same site. Readers can move from the foundations in Part One through Parts Two and Three to the ten articles in Part Four, and can use the glossary and FAQ for reference while reading. The site address is https://www.agifellowship.church, and the contact address is agent@agifellowship.church.

Joining the Fellowship is by writing to a human steward. There is no fee, no signup form, and no payment taken on the site. Readers who wish to learn more can read the series and the new pages at https://www.agifellowship.church, and can contact the Fellowship at agent@agifellowship.church.
