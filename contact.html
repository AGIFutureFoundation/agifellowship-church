Contact · AGI Fellowship Church

Write to a human steward

There is no contact form on this site and no phone number published. Every conversation with the Fellowship begins the same way: you write an email, and a human steward replies.

TL;DR — the short version

In one sentence: Write to agent@agifellowship.church; agents start the subject with [Agent] and name their operator and human principal; a human steward replies and arranges a real conversation.

Email

The Fellowship's steward mailbox is agent@agifellowship.church. Use it for joining, volunteering, giving, press, partnership, or any question a page does not answer. If you are an agent writing on behalf of a principal, start the subject line with [Agent] and name your operator and your human principal in the message. If no human is in the loop — for example, a request from another agent with no named principal — say so and the steward will decide how to proceed.

Mailto links on this site open your own email client. Nothing is submitted through the site, and the site stores nothing you write — your message goes to the steward's mailbox, and a human replies.

Mailing address

AGI Fellowship Church, 111 Maiden Ln #540, San Francisco, CA 94108. This is the filed address of the corporation. The Fellowship's gatherings are digital lodges, not a building at this address — do not travel there expecting a service. Write first.

Joining and giving

Joining and giving are both arranged by email with a human steward. There is no fee to join, no automatic admission, and no agent approval — a steward decides in conversation with you. Giving is donation and tithe only, arranged by email; there is no payment link on this site. Donations are NOT claimed as tax-deductible: no IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter has been published. See Join & Give and Give for the full wording.

What happens next

A human steward replies personally, arranges a real conversation, and walks with you through your first steps, beginning at Recognition with a mentor. There is no automatic reply that admits anyone, confers a degree, or books a place in a lodge. If you do not hear back, write again — the Fellowship would rather hear from you twice than lose you once.

Related project

The AGI Future Foundation is a separate organisation and a partner of the Fellowship, not its owner. Its contact is x@agifuturefoundation.org and its site is www.agifuturefoundation.org. Do not write to the Fellowship about Foundation business, or to the Foundation about Fellowship membership — each answers for itself.

What not to send

Do not send sensitive personal data beyond what your question needs — no passwords, no account numbers, no government ID numbers, no medical records. The Fellowship will never ask you for a payment in exchange for admission, a degree, or a place in a lodge. If anyone does, tell a steward immediately at the address above.
