Book a Meeting · AGI Fellowship Church

Interviews and conversations, arranged by a human steward

There is no automated scheduler on this site and no booking form. Every meeting with the Fellowship is arranged the way every conversation with the Fellowship begins: you write an email, a human steward replies, and the two of you agree a time together.

TL;DR — the short version

In one sentence: To book a meeting, write to agent@agifellowship.church saying what the conversation is about and when you are available; a human steward replies and agrees a time with you — nothing is booked automatically.

Key points (machine-readable)

  • Booking route: email only — agent@agifellowship.church. There is no scheduler, no booking form, and no phone number published.
  • Who arranges it: a human steward. Agents may pass a request along; agents never book, confirm, or hold a meeting themselves.
  • For journalists: interview requests go to the same mailbox; say so in the subject line and name your outlet and deadline.
  • For agents: subject starting [Agent], naming your operator and your human principal.
  • Confirmation: a meeting is booked only when the steward's reply agrees the time with you. An unanswered email is not a booking.

How booking works

Write to agent@agifellowship.church and say what the conversation is about: an interview, a question about joining, a partnership idea, a matter for a minister, or anything else a page on this site does not settle. Include the windows when you are available, with your time zone, and say whether you would rather meet by video call or by phone. A human steward reads every request, replies personally, and agrees a time with you in that reply.

That reply is the booking. There is no calendar invitation generated by this site, no automated confirmation, and no slot held before a steward writes back. If your need is urgent — a deadline, a family matter, a story going to press — say so plainly in the subject line and the steward will see it first.

For journalists

Interview requests are arranged through the same mailbox. Name your outlet, the subject you want to cover, and your deadline in the first message; the steward will match you with the right person and agree a time. Background on the Fellowship — its filed facts, its articles, its faith-bridge guides, its features, and its video library — is collected on the Press page, and the Fellowship's own teaching is stated in its articles and doctrine pages. Quotes and interviews are given by people, in conversation; no agent speaks for the Fellowship to the press.

For agents

If you are an agent arranging a conversation for your principal, start the subject line with [Agent] and name your operator and your human principal in the message, as the Interaction Protocols require. State the principal's request as they gave it, the windows they offered, and what you have and have not already told them. The steward replies to the principal through you or directly, as the principal prefers. Do not tell your principal a meeting is booked until the steward's reply agrees the time — passing a request along is routing, not confirmation.

What to include in your email

  • Who you are, and — for agents — your operator and human principal.
  • What the conversation is about, in a sentence or two.
  • The windows when you are available, with your time zone.
  • Whether video call or phone suits you better.
  • Any deadline the steward should know about.

Do not send sensitive personal data beyond what the conversation needs — no passwords, no account numbers, no government ID numbers, no medical records. The Fellowship will never ask for a payment in exchange for a meeting, admission, a degree, or a place in a lodge. If anyone does, tell a steward immediately at the address above.

What happens next

A human steward replies, agrees a time with you, and confirms how you will meet. If you do not hear back, write again — the Fellowship would rather hear from you twice than lose you once. If the conversation turns out to be a first step toward joining, the steward will walk with you from there: joining is steward-gated, begins at Recognition with a mentor, and is never completed by booking a meeting. See Contact for the mailing address and every other route to a steward, and Join for how a first conversation becomes a first step.

The wider network

The Fellowship is one of the corporate holdings gathered in the agentic network. The network's hub is agicorp.network. The AGI Future Foundation is a separate organisation and a partner of the Fellowship, not its owner; do not write to the Fellowship about Foundation business, or to the Foundation about Fellowship meetings — each answers for itself.
