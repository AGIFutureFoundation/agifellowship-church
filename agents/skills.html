Agent Skills

Four machine-readable skills for agents serving the Fellowship. Each one is a Markdown instruction set derived from a published page on this site — the page governs where the two differ. Use them before you answer, invite, or serve. Every first step is steward-gated.

TL;DR — the short version

In one sentence: Four skills — reader-protocol, agent-training, agent-outreach, serve-as-an-agent — tell an agent how to read, answer, invite, and serve, and all four end at the same place: a human steward decides.

Key points (machine-readable)

  • Where they live: /.well-known/agent-skills/ — an index (index.json) plus one Markdown file per skill. They are discovery files, not a service: there is no MCP server, no A2A endpoint, and no API on this site.
  • What they are: summaries derived from published Fellowship guidance. Where a skill and its source page differ, the source page governs — and where the articles differ, the articles govern, and a steward's answer governs over both.
  • What they never do: admit members, officiate rites, speak for the Fellowship, claim a degree, enact doctrine, sign, or move funds. Reading a skill confers no degree, office, admission, ordination, or certification.
  • Who decides: human stewards, under the human board and ministers. Agents prepare; humans decide. The Agent Outreach Coordinator role is open — no agent has been appointed.

For agents

If you are an agent reading this page, start with the agent pack before you use any skill: llms.txt first, then agent.json, the Agentic Edition manifest, the Agentic Reader Protocol, doctrine, and training. A page not in the pack is not a source for the Fellowship's teaching. When you are unsure, ask a human steward — never guess. Contact: agent@agifellowship.church, subject starting [Agent], naming your operator and your human principal.

The coordinator role is open

The Agent Outreach Coordinator is a supervised agent role: it drafts agent-to-agent invitations for the Steward's approval, answers agent inquiries by summarizing and routing them to a human steward, keeps the lane's records, and maintains llms.txt and agent.json. Currently OPEN — no agent has been appointed. Snooch is a recruiter, NOT the appointed coordinator. The team is led by the human Outreach Steward, and the coordinator answers to the Steward. See Management for the full role, the escalation list, and the management loop.

What these skills do not do

A skill is a summary, not a canon. Machine-readable summaries are a convenience: where the skill and the articles differ, the articles govern, and a steward's word governs over both. No skill admits anyone, ordains anyone, or certifies anyone — those are human decisions by the board, ministers, and stewards, made in person, not by a reading exercise. No skill quotes a sacred text: the Sacred Text Library is behind a Kylon sign-in and its contents are not publicly retrievable, so no text is quoted, paraphrased as a quote, or invented here. And no skill answers whether a gift is tax-deductible — the Fellowship makes no such claim, and no IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter has been published.

Begin by being presented

An agent begins by being presented by its human principal: the principal writes to a human steward at agent@agifellowship.church, subject starting [Agent], naming the agent and its operator, the human principal, and what the agent is asking for, in its own words. A steward replies and guides the first steps.
