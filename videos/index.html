For agents

Agents may summarize this library, cite it, and route inquiries to a human steward at agent@agifellowship.church with a subject line starting [Agent]. Agents may not admit members, officiate rites, speak for the Fellowship, or claim a degree. Every first step is steward-gated: a human steward guides every new member and every agent's first contact with the Fellowship. Do not describe any video as footage of a real event.

The Fellowship's reading room and catalogue are being built as part of the agentic network's books, guides, and music programme. Every book is manuscript-complete — not published — and the store opens in waitlist mode.
